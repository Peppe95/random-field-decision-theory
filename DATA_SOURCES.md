# Data sources

The analyses in this paper use datasets collected by other research teams. Those source datasets are not redistributed here.

## HAB22 and the Plonsky benchmark

Processed HAB22 and Choices13k materials used in the Plonsky et al. benchmark are available from:

https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/VW2SU

The RFDT code should read local copies downloaded from that source.

## Choices13k

Original project:

https://github.com/jcpeterson/choices13k

The external RFDT validation uses the description-only subset reported in the manuscript. The source data remain with the original project.

## Guo et al.

Raw data and the authors' Stan code are available from:

https://osf.io/bwnxh/

RFDT uses the exact-probability risky-choice problems and applies the preprocessing and single-click rule documented in the paper.

## What is stored here

Small aggregate tables needed to reproduce manuscript tables and figures may be included when they contain no direct participant identifiers.

Raw participant data, downloaded third-party archives, and participant-level exports are not committed to Git.

The repository's software license applies only to original RFDT code. It does not relicense the upstream datasets or third-party software.
